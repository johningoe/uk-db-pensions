DB Scheme Gaps

Every live DB scheme with no usable figure, and every scheme still a candidate for the £50m–£500m band: why, what has been tried, and the next step. Identified employers are one row each; schemes with no employer found are one row per scheme. Open rows could be settled by work. Assumed rows are out of the band on an assumption, and a fixed sample of each assumption is picked out to test it. Explained rows are out for a reason that needs no work (dissolved, no DB scheme in the accounts, outside the band on review).
